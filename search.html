⚙️ How to Connect Your Programmable Search Engine ID

Google Programmable Search Engine generates revenue through AdSense for Search (AFS) when visitors query your site.

  1. Visit Google Programmable Search Engine.
  2. Click Add, enter financeagentguide.com/* under "Sites to search", and name your engine.
  3. Navigate to Ads in the left sidebar and toggle Search Engine Monetization ON to link your AdSense account.
  4. Copy your Search Engine ID (e.g., 0123456789abcdef:xxxxxxx).
  5. In search.html, replace YOUR_SEARCH_ENGINE_ID with your copied ID.
